Critical Dependencies
All thirteen Vitality Index indicators are sourced exclusively via the Daystar University data-access partnership. No fallback ingestion path exists. If Daystar cannot deliver an indicator, that indicator stays unscored (handled cleanly via partial-score logic — ScoreCalculator excludes nulls from pillar averages). Phase B cannot reach full completion until Daystar delivery is confirmed across all four pillars. This is the single largest schedule risk in the project.
Vitality Index Structure
All pillars are weighted equally (0.25 each). All legacy metrics (SPI, Optimal Density Ratio, Urban Friction Index, ESIA Transparency, Digital Sovereignty, etc.) are fully REMOVED. Do not reference them.
The Four Pillars & Thirteen Indicators
Pillar 1 — Social Wellbeing and Human Capital (Weight: 0.25)
* Healthcare access
* Education access
* Digital connectivity
Pillar 2 — Safety and Security (Weight: 0.25)
* Crime rates
* Emergency response access
* Disaster exposure
Pillar 3 — Density and Scaling Dynamics (Weight: 0.25)
* Population density
* Congestion
* Housing pressure
Pillar 4 — Infrastructure and Environmental Safeguards (Weight: 0.25)
* Road quality
* Energy reliability
* Food risk
* Waste management
Scoring Engine Logic
* Normalize each indicator on a strict scale of 0 -> 100.
* Pillar Score = Average of its normalized indicators (non-null only).
* Composite Score = Average of the four pillar scores (skips fully-null pillars).
* Missing Data Handling: Exclude missing indicators from the average calculation entirely (never treat them as zero). Mark the overall score as incomplete if any indicator is absent. See App\Services\ScoreCalculator::pillarScores + pillarScoresFromValues (the latter used by ZoneHistoryController when averaging snapshot columns).
* Visibility: Log exactly which indicators are missing per locality via ScoreCalculator::missingIndicators — powers the "9 of 13 indicators active" UI badge.
